001方向 01 · 自主可控 AIFrontier 01 · Sovereign AI2026-11-15模型：Dory 4B，开源Model: Dory 4B, open weights

Dory：不让模型多写字，让它在里面多想几遍Dory: instead of writing more, the model thinks a few more times inside

科学问题
想让模型答得更好，主流做法是让它写更长的思维链，代价是更慢、更占显存。能不能不多写字，也让小模型变强？
创新点
72 层拆成读题 16 层、思考 40 层、作答 16 层，中间 40 层可以反复跑 1 到 4 遍。跑 4 遍相当于 192 层在工作，参数仍是 4B。同一份权重，简单题跑 1 遍，难题跑 4 遍。
结果
12 个榜单平均分：1 遍 35.2，2 遍 39.7，4 遍 40.6，跑两遍就超过 Qwen3.5-9B（32.2）。SWE-Bench Verified 从 50.3 升到 61.8。GPQA 上仍输给 Qwen3.5-9B（72.2 对 80.9），4 遍的计算量约是 1 遍的 2.7 倍。
源头论文
“同一层反复用”来自 2018 年的 Universal Transformers；近两年有工作从理论上解释循环为什么帮推理，也有人在预训练阶段就开始循环。
机构
NVIDIA
启发
除了把模型做大、让它多写字，还有第三个旋钮：在里面多算几遍。对手机和便宜显卡这种跑不动大模型的地方，这条路最有用，也正是自主可控 AI 关心的场景。
The question
The usual way to make a model answer better is a longer chain of thought, which costs time and memory. Can a small model get stronger without writing more?
What's new
Its 72 layers split into 16 for reading, 40 for thinking and 16 for answering. The middle 40 can run 1 to 4 times. Four passes act like a 192-layer model with the same 4B parameters. One set of weights: one pass for easy questions, four for hard ones.
Results
Average over 12 benchmarks: 35.2 with one pass, 39.7 with two, 40.6 with four. Two passes already beat Qwen3.5-9B (32.2). SWE-Bench Verified rises from 50.3 to 61.8. It still trails Qwen3.5-9B on GPQA (72.2 vs 80.9), and four passes cost about 2.7 times the compute of one.
Where it comes from
Reusing the same layers goes back to Universal Transformers (2018). Recent work explains in theory why looping helps reasoning, and some groups loop from pre-training onward.
Who
NVIDIA
Why it matters
Beyond making models bigger or making them write more, there is a third dial: compute more times internally. It helps most where big models can't run, such as phones and cheap GPUs, which is exactly where sovereign AI lives.

火种记忆格式Fireseed Memory format草案 v0.1Draft v0.1

一个开放、可携带、可离线的 AI 记忆格式。记忆是纯文本，一行一条，换模型、换厂商时整个带走。An open, portable, offline format for AI memory. Memories are plain text, one per line, and move with you when you change models or vendors.

  • 每条记忆记下来源：出自哪次对话、哪个模型
  • 向量索引只是可重建的缓存，换模型记忆不变
  • 遗忘就是从文件里删掉，不是打隐藏标记
  • 规范与参考工具按 Apache-2.0 开源，正在整理后公开
  • Every memory records its source: which conversation, which model
  • Vector indexes are a rebuildable cache, so switching models leaves memories intact
  • Forgetting removes the line from the file instead of hiding it
  • Spec and reference tool under Apache-2.0, being prepared for release
